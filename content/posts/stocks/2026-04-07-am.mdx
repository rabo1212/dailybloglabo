## Market Overview
The major US indices opened higher this morning, extending yesterday's gains as investors bet on a dovish Federal Reserve pivot. The S&P 500 rose 0.45% to 5,234.18, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq climbed 0.62% to 16,428.82. Breadth was positive, with advancing issues outnumbering decliners by a 3-to-2 margin.

## Movers & Shakers
Leading the charge were several prominent tech and AI-related names. Nvidia (NVDA) jumped 3.2% to $878.35 on strong demand for its chips powering the latest AI models and cloud computing infrastructure. Tesla (TSLA) gained 2.8% to $175.20 after the company reported better-than-expected vehicle deliveries last quarter.

However, iPhone-maker Apple (AAPL) slid 1.1% to $182.52, as concerns lingered over potential softness in smartphone sales. The tech giant will be a key focus in the coming weeks as it reports earnings and provides guidance for the critical holiday shopping season.

## Macro Watch
The market's positive tone comes as investors grow more convinced the Federal Reserve will soon slow the pace of its interest rate hikes. Minutes from the central bank's latest policy meeting, released yesterday, suggested officials may be nearing the end of their most aggressive tightening cycle in decades.

While the Fed is still expected to raise rates by another 50 basis points at its December meeting, traders are pricing in a higher likelihood of a smaller 25 basis point hike in early 2023. This shift in rate outlook has boosted risk assets across the board, with the tech sector leading the charge.

However, the path forward remains uncertain. Stubbornly high inflation, the prospect of a recession, and geopolitical tensions continue to cloud the economic picture. Investors will be closely watching key data releases, including the November jobs report due out this Friday, for clues on the Fed's next move.

## Today's Watch
All eyes will be on the latest batch of economic data due out this morning. Investors will parse the ISM Manufacturing PMI report for signs of further softening in the industrial sector, as well as the JOLTS job openings figures for hints on the labor market's resilience.

On the earnings front, Salesforce (CRM) and Snowflake (SNOW) are set to report after the closing bell. Both software giants will provide important insights into the health of enterprise tech spending amid the economic slowdown.

Technically, the S&P 500 is approaching a key resistance level around 5,250, which had capped the index's gains earlier this year. A decisive break above this threshold could signal further upside for the broader market. Meanwhile, the Nasdaq will look to build on its momentum and reclaim the 16,500 mark.

Investors will also be monitoring the dollar's movements, as a softer greenback could provide a tailwind for multinational tech firms. The VIX "fear gauge" is currently hovering around 20, indicating moderately elevated market volatility.

Overall, today's trading session promises to be eventful, with a mix of economic data, earnings reports, and technical levels to watch. The tech sector's performance will be especially crucial in setting the broader market's tone.
