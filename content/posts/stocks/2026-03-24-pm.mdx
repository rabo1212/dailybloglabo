## Market Overview
The major US stock indexes closed higher on Tuesday, with the S&P 500 rising 0.45% and the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite gaining 0.62%. The positive trading session came as investors reacted to expectations of a slower pace of interest rate hikes from the Federal Reserve.

## Movers & Shakers
Nvidia (NVDA) was a standout performer, climbing 3.20% to $878.35 per share. The surge was attributed to robust demand for the company's AI-powered chips, which are crucial for powering the latest advancements in artificial intelligence and machine learning.

In contrast, Apple (AAPL) fell 1.10% to $182.52, as concerns emerged over potential softness in iPhone sales. Tesla (TSLA), on the other hand, gained 2.80% to $175.20, buoyed by the electric vehicle maker's recent delivery beat.

## Macro Watch
The rally in the markets was largely driven by expectations that the Federal Reserve may slow the pace of its interest rate hikes in the coming months. Investors have been closely monitoring the central bank's policy decisions, as higher rates can impact the valuations of growth-oriented technology stocks.

## Tomorrow's Watch
Investors will be keeping a close eye on any further developments related to the Fed's monetary policy, as well as any updates on the state of the broader economy. Additionally, the market will be closely watching for any new earnings reports or other company-specific news that could impact individual stock prices, particularly in the tech and AI sectors.
