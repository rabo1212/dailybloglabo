## Market Overview
The tech-heavy Nasdaq composite rallied 0.62% this morning, outpacing the S&P 500's 0.45% gain, as investors shrugged off concerns over the Federal Reserve's monetary policy path. Breadth was positive, with over 60% of Nasdaq stocks trading higher.

## Movers & Shakers
Leading the charge was NVIDIA (NVDA), which jumped 3.2% to $878.35 on reports of surging demand for its AI-focused chips. The company's data center business has been a key growth driver, with revenue from that segment up 88% year-over-year last quarter. Analysts expect NVIDIA to continue capitalizing on the explosion of generative AI applications like ChatGPT.

Tesla (TSLA) also gained 2.8% to $175.20 after the electric vehicle maker reported stronger-than-expected vehicle deliveries for the first quarter. While the 255,000 units delivered fell short of the company's 2023 guidance, it was an improvement from the previous quarter and eased concerns about demand headwinds.

In contrast, Apple (AAPL) slipped 1.1% to $182.52 amid worries about slowing iPhone sales. The tech giant is expected to unveil its next-generation iPhone this fall, but investors are growing cautious about the smartphone market's trajectory in the face of macroeconomic uncertainty.

## Macro Watch
The market's positive sentiment today comes as investors scale back expectations for aggressive interest rate hikes from the Federal Reserve. Minutes from the central bank's March meeting, released yesterday, suggested policymakers may pause rate increases at an upcoming meeting to assess the impact of prior tightening.

This has eased concerns about the risk of a recession, though the economy remains on shaky ground. The latest jobs report showed the U.S. added a robust 236,000 jobs in March, exceeding forecasts, but wage growth moderated slightly, providing the Fed some room to hold off on further rate hikes.

Geopolitical tensions also remain in focus, with ongoing uncertainty around the Russia-Ukraine conflict. However, markets have generally shrugged off these concerns in recent weeks, focusing more on the potential for a soft economic landing.

## Today's Watch
Investors will be closely watching the latest inflation data, with the March Consumer Price Index (CPI) report due out this morning. Economists expect the headline CPI to have risen 0.2% month-over-month, decelerating from February's 0.4% increase. Any surprises, either to the upside or downside, could significantly impact market sentiment and Fed policy expectations.

Additionally, several major tech companies are scheduled to report earnings this week, including Delta Air Lines, JPMorgan Chase, and Bed Bath & Beyond. These results will provide valuable insights into consumer demand, supply chain dynamics, and the broader economic landscape.

From a technical perspective, the Nasdaq Composite is approaching its 200-day moving average around 16,500, a key resistance level that could determine the near-term direction of the tech sector. Traders will be monitoring how the market reacts to this potential inflection point.

Overall, the markets' optimistic tone suggests investors are growing more confident in the economy's ability to withstand higher interest rates. However, the path forward remains uncertain, and volatility is likely to persist as market participants weigh the competing forces of cooling inflation, the Fed's policy decisions, and the broader macroeconomic environment.
