## Market Overview
The tech-heavy Nasdaq index climbed 0.62% today, outpacing the broader S&P 500's 0.45% gain. Bullish sentiment around AI-driven stocks like Nvidia offset lingering concerns over iPhone demand at Apple. Overall, the market kept up its recent momentum as investors eagerly await the Federal Reserve's next move on interest rates.

## Movers & Shakers
Nvidia (NVDA) was a standout performer, rising 3.2% to $878.35. The surge came on reports of surging demand for the company's cutting-edge AI chips, which are powering the latest breakthroughs in areas like natural language processing and generative AI. Nvidia's dominance in the AI hardware space makes it a prime beneficiary of the ongoing AI boom, and Wall Street is taking notice.

In contrast, Apple (AAPL) slipped 1.1% to $182.52 amid ongoing concerns about iPhone sales. While the tech giant recently reported better-than-expected earnings, there are worries that consumer demand for its flagship smartphone may be starting to cool. With the iPhone still accounting for the majority of Apple's revenue, any softness in that business could weigh on the company's overall performance.

Tesla (TSLA) rose 2.8% to $175.20 after the electric vehicle maker reported stronger-than-expected Q3 deliveries. The results suggest Tesla is continuing to navigate supply chain challenges better than its rivals, cementing its position as the market leader in EVs. Investors will be watching closely to see if the company can maintain its delivery momentum in the face of growing competition.

## Macro Watch
The rally in tech stocks comes as investors grow increasingly confident that the Federal Reserve will slow the pace of its interest rate hikes. Minutes from the central bank's September meeting, released this week, indicated that policymakers are considering a more gradual approach to tightening monetary policy in the months ahead.

This shift in expectations has helped buoy risk assets like equities, which had been under pressure earlier this year as the Fed embarked on its most aggressive rate hike campaign in decades. With inflation showing some signs of cooling, the central bank may have more flexibility to take its foot off the gas, a prospect that the market is clearly embracing.

At the same time, the labor market remains tight, with the unemployment rate holding steady at 3.5% in September. This dynamic could complicate the Fed's efforts to engineer a "soft landing" for the economy, as strong employment could stoke further inflationary pressures.

Geopolitical tensions also continue to simmer, with the war in Ukraine dragging on and US-China relations remaining strained. These geopolitical risks could introduce additional volatility into the market in the months ahead.

## Tomorrow's Watch
All eyes will be on the latest inflation data, with the September consumer price index (CPI) report due out on Thursday. Economists are expecting a year-over-year increase of 8.1%, down from 8.3% in August. Any deviation from these forecasts could have a significant impact on market sentiment and the Fed's policy calculus.

Investors will also be closely monitoring the ongoing earnings season, with a number of high-profile tech companies scheduled to report results in the coming days and weeks. Particular attention will be paid to guidance from these firms, as Wall Street seeks clues about the health of the broader economy.

Additionally, the market will be on the lookout for any further developments in the AI space, given the outsized influence of companies like Nvidia. As the technology continues to evolve and find new applications, the race to dominate this critical field is sure to be a major driver of stock performance.

Overall, the tech-centric rally witnessed today suggests that investors remain cautiously optimistic about the market's prospects, even as they navigate a complex macroeconomic landscape. The coming days and weeks will be crucial in determining whether this momentum can be sustained.
