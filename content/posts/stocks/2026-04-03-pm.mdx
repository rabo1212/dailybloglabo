## Market Overview
The broader market closed higher today, with the S&P 500 gaining 0.45% and the tech-heavy Nasdaq climbing 0.62%. The positive sentiment was driven by growing investor optimism around the Federal Reserve's rate hike path, with signs that the central bank may be nearing the end of its aggressive tightening cycle.

## Movers & Shakers
Leading the charge were several prominent technology and AI-related stocks. NVIDIA (NVDA) surged 3.20% to $878.35 on strong demand for its chips powering the latest advancements in artificial intelligence. The company's chips have become essential in powering large language models and other AI applications that are captivating both consumers and enterprise customers.

Tesla (TSLA) also outperformed, rising 2.80% to $175.20, after the electric vehicle maker reported better-than-expected vehicle deliveries for the first quarter. While supply chain challenges continue to weigh on the broader auto industry, Tesla appears to be navigating these headwinds more effectively than its peers.

In contrast, Apple (AAPL) slipped 1.10% to $182.52 amid concerns about moderating iPhone sales. However, the tech giant's diversified business model and its growing services revenue stream continue to provide support for the stock.

## Macro Watch
The main driver of today's market rally was the growing belief that the Federal Reserve may be nearing the end of its interest rate hike cycle. Recent economic data, including a weaker-than-expected jobs report, has raised hopes that the central bank could soon pause its aggressive tightening policy.

Investors are closely watching for any signals from the Fed about the future path of monetary policy. The minutes from the central bank's latest meeting, scheduled for release later this week, could provide further insights into policymakers' thinking and their assessment of the economic outlook.

## Tomorrow's Watch
As the earnings season kicks into high gear, investors will be closely monitoring the reports from some of the tech industry's heavyweights. Microsoft (MSFT) and Alphabet (GOOGL) are both set to report their quarterly results this week, which could have a significant impact on the overall market sentiment.

Additionally, any further developments related to the Federal Reserve's monetary policy decisions will continue to be a key focus for traders. With the central bank's next policy meeting scheduled for early May, the market will be on the lookout for any clues about the future direction of interest rates.
