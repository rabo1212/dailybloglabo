## Market Overview
The major US indices opened the trading week in positive territory, with the tech-heavy Nasdaq leading the charge. The S&P 500 gained 0.45% to 5,234.18, while the Nasdaq composite rose 0.62% to 16,428.82. Despite the upbeat start, lingering macroeconomic concerns continue to weigh on investor sentiment.

## Movers & Shakers
A surge in demand for AI-related technologies was a key driver of strength in the tech sector. Nvidia (NVDA) saw its shares jump 3.2% to $878.35 as the company continues to benefit from the growing appetite for its advanced chips used in AI training and inference. Meanwhile, Tesla (TSLA) also gained 2.8% to $175.20 after the electric vehicle maker reported better-than-expected delivery numbers for the previous quarter.

However, not all tech giants were immune to the broader market jitters. Apple (AAPL) dipped 1.1% to $182.52 as investors fretted over potential softness in iPhone sales amid a challenging macroeconomic environment.

## Macro Watch
The markets' rally was largely driven by growing expectations of a slower pace of interest rate hikes from the Federal Reserve. Recent economic data, including a weaker-than-expected jobs report, has fueled hopes that the central bank may be nearing the end of its aggressive tightening cycle. This has provided a boost to risk assets, with investors betting on a more dovish Fed stance going forward.

That said, underlying concerns about the broader economic outlook remain. Geopolitical tensions, high inflation, and the potential for a recession continue to cast a shadow over the markets, limiting the upside potential.

## Today's Watch
As the trading session unfolds, investors will be closely monitoring the performance of AI-driven technology stocks, which have become a focal point for the market. Key levels to watch include Nvidia's ability to maintain its momentum and potentially challenge its all-time high, as well as Tesla's ability to build on its recent delivery beat.

Additionally, any further insights from the Federal Reserve and its policy decisions will be closely scrutinized, as the market attempts to gauge the central bank's next move and its potential impact on the broader economic landscape.
