## Market Overview
The S&P 500 and Nasdaq opened the day in positive territory, up 0.45% and 0.62% respectively. The tech-heavy Nasdaq is leading the charge, driven by strong performance in AI-related names.

## Movers & Shakers
NVIDIA (NVDA) is up 3.20% to $878.35 on the back of surging demand for its AI chips. The company is seen as a key beneficiary of the growing adoption of artificial intelligence across industries.

Tesla (TSLA) is also gaining, up 2.80% to $175.20, after the electric vehicle maker reported better-than-expected delivery numbers.

However, Apple (AAPL) is bucking the trend, trading 1.10% lower at $182.52 amid concerns over iPhone sales.

## Macro Watch
The broader market rally is being fueled by growing expectations that the Federal Reserve may slow the pace of interest rate hikes going forward. Investors are closely monitoring any signals from the central bank on its monetary policy outlook.

## Today's Watch
As the trading session unfolds, key levels to watch include the S&P 500's ability to hold above the 5,200 mark and the Nasdaq's push towards new highs. Investors will also be keenly focused on any further developments related to the AI demand surge and its impact on tech stocks.
