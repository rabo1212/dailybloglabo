## Lead Story

Anthropic, the AI research company behind the popular ChatGPT assistant, has abruptly ended its OpenClaw program, which allowed developers to access the company's large language model Claude for free. This surprise move signals a shift in Anthropic's strategy as it focuses on monetizing its AI technology through new paid subscription tiers.

The OpenClaw program, launched last year, enabled developers to experiment with and integrate the Claude model into their applications. However, Anthropic has now notified users that this free access will be discontinued, effective immediately. This decision comes just as the company unveiled its new "Pro," "Max," and "Team" paid plans for Claude, which offer varying levels of usage allowances and features.

The termination of OpenClaw is a clear indication that Anthropic is prioritizing revenue generation over its previous focus on developer engagement and experimentation. By restricting access to Claude and pushing users toward its paid offerings, the company aims to capture more of the value it believes its AI technology can provide. This strategic shift aligns with the broader trend in the AI industry, where companies are increasingly seeking to commercialize their models and turn them into sustainable business ventures.
