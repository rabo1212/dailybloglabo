## Lead Story
The AI community was abuzz today with the debut of ProofShot, a novel system that aims to bring visual verification capabilities to AI coding agents. ProofShot allows these agents to "see" the user interfaces they generate and validate their output against design requirements. This advancement could be a game-changer for ensuring the fidelity of AI-produced code and user experiences, addressing a key pain point as these technologies become more widespread. By equipping coding AIs with eyes to inspect their own work, ProofShot introduces an important safeguard against errors or unintended visual outcomes.

Another standout story was the demonstration of an iPhone 17 Pro running a 400 billion parameter large language model (LLM). This feat highlights the rapid progress in deploying massive AI models on consumer hardware, a trend that will likely accelerate as hardware and software optimizations continue. The implications stretch beyond mobile devices, suggesting that even more powerful LLMs could soon be feasible on a wide range of edge devices, transforming the landscape of AI-powered applications.
